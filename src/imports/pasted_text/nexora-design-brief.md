Design a premium, modern cryptocurrency fintech platform called “NEXORA” — a fictional crypto investment and portfolio management platform for a new generation of digital asset users.

The design should feel like a real funded fintech startup: trustworthy, sophisticated, innovative, and highly polished. Avoid the generic “crypto casino” aesthetic. Prioritize clarity, usability, financial transparency, and strong visual hierarchy.

BRAND:
Name: NEXORA
Tagline: “Your assets. Your future. One intelligent platform.”
Industry: Cryptocurrency / Digital Assets / Fintech
Target users: Young professionals, crypto investors, digital entrepreneurs, and financially curious users aged 20–40.
Brand personality: Intelligent, secure, confident, modern, transparent.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Premium fintech aesthetic
- Dark-first interface
- Deep charcoal/near-black background
- Electric blue and subtle violet accents
- White and cool-gray typography
- Subtle gradients used sparingly
- Glassmorphism only where useful
- Soft shadows and thin borders
- Rounded cards, approximately 12–20px radius
- Clean financial dashboards similar in sophistication to modern fintech products
- Avoid excessive neon, floating coins, rockets, Bitcoin symbols, or cliché crypto imagery
- Use abstract geometric/3D visual elements instead of stock crypto graphics

TYPOGRAPHY:
Use Inter or another modern geometric sans-serif.
Create a clear hierarchy:
- Large bold display headings
- Medium-weight section headings
- Highly readable body text
- Monospace/technical styling for wallet addresses, transaction IDs, and numerical data where appropriate.
